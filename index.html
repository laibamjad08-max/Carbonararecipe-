Carbonara cooking tips

Take the pan off the heat

Add the egg mixture away from direct heat so it thickens gently. Full details are in our carbonara sauce guide.

Save the pasta water

Starchy water turns eggs and cheese into a glossy sauce. Keep a cup before you drain.

Grate the cheese finely

Fine cheese melts smoothly. Coarse shreds tend to clump, so see how to fix clumpy carbonara.
