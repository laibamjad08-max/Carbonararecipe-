Food safety

Traditional carbonara sauce is set by the heat of hot pasta and is not cooked like a custard. Use fresh, properly stored eggs. People who are pregnant, elderly, very young, or have a weakened immune system should be careful with eggs that are not fully cooked and may prefer to avoid them.

Allergies and diet

Carbonara contains eggs, dairy, and wheat. Check every ingredient label for allergens. Nothing here is medical or nutritional advice. Consult a qualified professional about your health needs.
